PRIVACY POLICY

Know where your data lives.

This policy explains information handling in the CushionLab app, its public support website and support email.

Updated and effective: October 29, 2026

CushionLab requires no separate account. The app does not upload your positions, notes or learning progress to a developer server, and has no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data on your device

Saved positions, notes, learning progress, preferences and daily free-shot usage are stored on the current device. The app has no developer-operated data collection service, third-party analytics SDK or in-app cloud sync. Your Apple backup settings determine whether local app data is included in device backups.

Pro purchases

Apple processes Pro payments. CushionLab uses StoreKit to load product prices and verify purchases, restoration, refunds and revocations. Verified transaction evidence determines Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's processing of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Managing and deleting local data

You can edit or delete individual positions in the library. Deleting the app removes its local data; system backups may retain copies according to your device and backup settings. Restoring Pro restores purchase access, not deleted positions. Apple provides restoration of existing device backups. You do not need to uninstall the app to restore a purchase.

This website and external links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you choose to open Apple terms, learning references or other external websites, their providers' privacy rules apply. Email services also operate separately from the app.

Contacting support

If you email justfret@163.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you provide and use them to handle your question or request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unrelated to the issue. You can also use this address for privacy questions or requests concerning your correspondence.

Policy updates

If future features change how information is handled, we will update this page, its effective date, the in-app privacy information and relevant App Store privacy disclosures.
