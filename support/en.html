HELP & SUPPORT

From a lesson to your own shot.

颗星实验室 is a Chinese eight-ball learning and practice companion for iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later.

Updated: October 29, 2026

Start practicing

  1. Explore an example in the Academy, or place the cue ball and object balls in Free practice.
  2. Drag behind the cue ball to aim the cue, adjust the power and red cue-tip point, then tap Strike.
  3. Pause or slow playback to study the route. Change one setting at a time and compare the results.
  4. Save a position and add notes from the tools menu. Bank shots also offers reference routes, lessons and challenges.

Free practice and Pro

What is included for free?

Lessons, automatic examples, route previews, ball placement and the position library remain available. Practice modes share a daily free-shot allowance shown in the app, refreshed when the local date advances. Pausing, resuming, previewing and saving do not consume another shot.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the daily shot limit across practice modes, with no subscription or automatic renewal. Apple's purchase confirmation shows the current price.

Why are some challenges still locked after buying Pro?

Challenges open as you progress through practice. Pro removes the daily shot limit; it does not skip lessons or complete challenges for you.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase in your device's Media & Purchases settings, connect to the internet, then open 颗星实验室's Pro page and select Restore purchases. Do not uninstall the app to restore Pro: uninstalling may delete local positions.

What if approval is pending, a purchase fails or no product appears?

You can close a pending purchase page and use any remaining free shots. The app updates access when Apple approves and delivers the transaction. If a request is declined, return to Pro to request it again. If a product is unavailable or a purchase fails, check your connection and Apple Account, then retry. Refunded or revoked transactions no longer grant Pro access.

Using the app and your data

Can I use it offline?

Practice and saved positions work offline. Verified purchases can use StoreKit's local transaction cache. First purchases and restoration on a new installation need an App Store connection. Visiting this website and sending email also need internet access.

Why does a real table behave differently?

Tables, cloth, cushions, ball condition and your stroke affect real results. 颗星实验室 helps explain and compare routes. It is not certified for real-table prediction and does not guarantee a real-table outcome.

Where are my saved positions?

Positions, notes and learning progress stay on the current device. There is no in-app account or cloud sync. You can edit or delete individual positions in the library. Restoring Pro restores purchase access, not deleted positions; Apple provides device-backup restoration.
